Tournament Rules

Tap on any round below to view the format, scoring, and specific rules.

Round 1: The Grind

▼

Format: Team Point Quota

Two-man teams, playing own ball. Both players’ scores on every hole contribute to the team total.

Scoring (Per Player)

  • Eagle or better: 5 pts
  • Birdie: 3 pts
  • Par: 2 pts
  • Bogey: 1 pts
  • Double or worse: 0 pts

Match Points

The team with the highest point total after 18 holes wins the match.

Win: 1 point | Tie: ½ point
Strict Rule: Pick up your ball if a bogey is not made. No mulligans.

Round 2: The Split Decision

▼

Format: 18-hole match split into two distinct 9-hole formats.

Points: Each 9-hole "side" is worth 1 point. Total of 2 points available per match.

Front 9: Two-Man Scramble

Captain’s Choice. Both players tee off, choose the best ball, and both play from that spot until holed.

  • Scoring: Stroke Play (total strokes for 9 holes).
  • Winner: The team with the lowest total strokes on the Front 9 wins 1 point.
Safety Net: Triple Bogey Max. If you haven't holed out by triple bogey, pick up and move to the next tee.

Back 9: Modified Alternate Shot

Greensomes. Both players tee off on every hole. The team chooses the best drive.

  • The Rotation: The player whose drive was NOT used hits the second shot. Players then alternate until the ball is holed.
  • Winner: The team with the lowest total strokes on the Back 9 wins 1 point.
Tee Ball Requirement: Each player must have at least 4 of their tee shots used during the 9-hole stretch.

Round 3: Championship Singles

▼

Format: Individual Head-to-Head Match Play. 8 matches total (Team A vs. Team B).

Scoring: Traditional Match Play. Holes are won, lost, or halved.

Points

Match Win: 1 point
Match Halved (Tie): ½ point
Match Loss: 0 points
Path to the Cup
Total points available (16 players):
4 + 4 + 4 + 8 = 20
10.5 points wins the Cup!

Tournament Tie-Breaker: "The Playoff"

▼

If the total team points are deadlocked after all three rounds, this format will be used to crown the champion.

Team Putting Contest

Each captain picks 3 players. Total made putts from 20 feet wins.

Sudden Death

If no putts are holed after 6 putts, a tee is flipped to determine who starts sudden death. Then, players alternate putts and the first holed ball wins the tournament.
