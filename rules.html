The Cup

Tournament rules

Formats, scoring and how the Cup gets decided. Tap any round to open it.

The 2027 formats will be posted once the captains are picked. Until then, these are the 2026 rules from Horseshoe Bay.

Universal rule: the “inside the leather” gimme

Any ball within the leather of your putter is a gimme: add one stroke to your score, no putt required.

Warning: if you decide to putt it anyway and miss, that stroke counts and the gimme is gone. Take the gimme or putt it. You can’t do both.

1

The Grind Team point quota · two-man teams

Format: Team point quota.

Two-man teams, each playing their own ball. Both players’ scores on every hole count toward the team total.

Scoring (per player)

  • Eagle or better: 5 pts
  • Birdie: 3 pts
  • Par: 2 pts
  • Bogey: 1 pt
  • Double or worse: 0 pts

Match points

The team with the most points after 18 holes wins the match.

Win: 1 pointTie: ½ point
Strict rule: Pick up once you can’t make bogey. No mulligans.
2

The Split Decision Front 9 scramble · back 9 alternate shot

Format: An 18-hole match split into two 9-hole formats.

Points: Each 9-hole side is worth 1 point, so each match is worth 2 points.

Front 9: two-man scramble

Captain’s choice. Both players tee off, pick the best ball, and both play from that spot until the ball is holed.

  • Scoring: Stroke play (total strokes for the nine).
  • Winner: Lowest total on the front 9 wins 1 point.
Safety net: Triple-bogey max. If you haven’t holed out by triple, pick up and move on.

Back 9: modified alternate shot

Greensomes. Both players tee off on every hole and the team picks the best drive.

  • The rotation: The player whose drive was not used hits the second shot, then the players alternate until the ball is holed.
  • Winner: Lowest total on the back 9 wins 1 point.
Tee ball requirement: Each player must have at least 4 of their drives used over the nine.
3

Championship Singles Head-to-head match play

Format: Individual head-to-head match play. 8 matches total, Team A vs. Team B.

Scoring: Traditional match play. Holes are won, lost or halved.

Points

Match win: 1 point Halved: ½ point Loss: 0 points
Path to the Cup
Total points available (16 players)
4 + 4 + 4 + 8 = 20
10½ points wins the Cup

The Playoff Tournament tie-breaker

If team points are tied after all three rounds, this decides the champion.

Team putting contest

Each captain picks 3 players. Most made putts from 20 feet wins.

Sudden death

If nobody holes a putt after 6 attempts, flip a tee to decide who starts sudden death. Players then alternate putts, and the first one to hole out wins the tournament.
